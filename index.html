The league that outlived the shield

The National Football League is gone. A last lockout, a broadcast collapse, and a sale that never closed left thirty-two cities without a Sunday.

From that wreckage a new circuit was chartered: the Alliance Football Association. Two conferences. Eight divisions. A clean sheet.

Every head coach is a free agent. Every player is unsigned. There are no inherited rosters.

The board named you, Jordan, to open the AFA — assign the sidelines, run the founding snake draft, and send the league into its first season.
