# AFA league culture and the 32 club cultures

This is fictional Alliance Football Association flavor. It is not a history of any real league, city government, or NFL club. Simulated traits on real players are game labels only. They are not biographies.

## League identity

Thirty-two cities chartered the AFA when they were left without a club. The first act of the league is the founding snake draft.

Tone: Civic and scrappy. The club belongs to the city more than to a broadcast brand.

Championship trophy: **Charter Cup**.

Rivalry week: **Anchor Week**. The last division weekend. Natural rivals meet, and the harbor bell, ridge drum, or main-street horn marks the kick.

Traditions:
- Founders' Night, the anniversary of the first draft
- A bell at the draft podium
- The Charter Cup walked through the host city's main street

## How culture plays

- CPU draft, trade, and free agency read the front office. Trade-aggressive and win-now clubs shop sooner. Frugal clubs spend less. Free-agent buyers pay more when the cap allows.
- Owner approval uses the club's patience. A win-now or short-patience owner wants a higher score. A patient owner rides a lower one.
- Each real player can carry one simulated trait (leader, film junkie, diva, mentor, or loner), chosen from the player id and labeled simulated. That trait fits or clashes with the club. It is not a real personality.
- Free agents prefer welcoming, glamorous, or exciting markets, then mid-size cities.
- Scheme fit compares the player's simulated scheme preference with the club's offense.
- After a season, a long winning run makes the building less patient, and a hard year pushes it back. A coaching change loosens the ritual. The city culture itself does not flip in one year.
